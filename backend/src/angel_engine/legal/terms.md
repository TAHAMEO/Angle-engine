> not legal advice. Have them reviewed by qualified counsel for your organization and jurisdiction
> before use.

## 1. What Angel Engine is

Angel Engine is an evidence-management and research platform for **authorized investigators** working
with **publicly available information**. It helps you collect, organize, verify and report on public
sources. It is **not** a people-search service and it does **not** perform facial recognition.

## 2. Who may use it

Access is granted by your organization's administrators after review. You may use Angel Engine only:

- for a **legitimate and lawful purpose** that you state when you create each investigation;
- within the scope of your role and any authorization your organization or the law requires;
- in compliance with the Acceptable Use Policy, which forms part of these terms.

## 3. Your responsibilities

- Provide truthful purpose statements and attestations. They are recorded and audited.
- Keep your credentials and authenticator device secure. Report suspected compromise immediately.
- Treat everything in an investigation as confidential to your organization.
- Verify findings before relying on them. Automated and AI outputs can be wrong; Angel Engine labels
  them as hypotheses for that reason.
- Respect the terms of the public sources you access through Angel Engine.

## 4. What Angel Engine will not do

Angel Engine will not bypass logins, paywalls, CAPTCHAs or other access controls; will not collect from
private accounts, leaked or breached datasets; will not identify people from their faces; and will not
reveal private contact details, home addresses, government identifiers, financial or medical
information. Requests whose purpose is any of these are refused.

## 5. Monitoring and enforcement

All actions are recorded in a tamper-evident audit log. Requests are screened against the Acceptable
Use Policy. Repeated or serious violations lead to suspension, revocation of access and, where
appropriate, referral to your organization or authorities.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Uploaded images and investigation data are kept only as long as necessary and are deleted according to
the retention settings described in the Privacy Policy. Deleted investigations are cryptographically
erased.

## 7. No warranty

Angel Engine is provided "as is". Public sources may be incomplete, outdated or wrong. You remain
responsible for your conclusions and for how you use them.

## 8. Changes

When these terms change, you will be asked to accept the new version before continuing.
