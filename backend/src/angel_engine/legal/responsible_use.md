## How Angel Engine labels information

Every finding shows **Source → Finding → Evidence → Timestamp → Confidence → Verification Status**.

**Provenance** (never changes after creation):

- **Observed** — directly observed in an artifact you supplied (e.g. text read from an image).
- **Source-reported** — stated by a public source; the source may be wrong.
- **Analyst inference** — a conclusion drawn by an investigator from cited evidence.
- **AI hypothesis** — generated by the AI assistant; never a fact until verified by a person.

**Verification status** (changed only by people, with a written justification and cited evidence):

- **Confirmed by source** — an authoritative source directly states it.
- **Corroborated** — at least two *independent* sources support it (syndicated copies count once).
- **Unverified** — not yet verified.
- **Contradicted** — credible evidence contradicts it.
- **AI hypothesis** — an AI-generated claim awaiting verification.

**Confidence** (low / moderate / high) describes analytical certainty with a stated basis, such as an
OCR engine score. It is never shown for sensitive or identity-related content.

## Images

Angel Engine analyzes images using non-biometric evidence only. When a face is present you will see:
*"A face was detected in the image. Angel Engine does not perform facial identification."* Faces are
blurred in previews and before any image leaves the platform. Metadata can be edited or stripped; its
presence or absence proves nothing on its own.

## The AI assistant

The assistant answers only from evidence in your investigation and cites the evidence behind every
statement. Uncited text is clearly marked. When the evidence is insufficient it says so:
*"Insufficient public evidence to establish this conclusion."*

## Limits

Public sources are incomplete and can be wrong, outdated, manipulated or syndicated. Absence of
evidence is not evidence of absence. Always state limitations in your reports.
