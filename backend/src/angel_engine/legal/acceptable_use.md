Angel Engine exists to **investigate public evidence, verify sources, protect privacy, and never turn an
AI inference into an identity claim.**

## Permitted uses

- Verifying the provenance of images and media using non-biometric clues (text, logos, landmarks,
  signage, metadata, publicly indexed copies).
- Researching organizations, websites, domains and publicly documented events.
- Journalism, fact-checking and misinformation research.
- Corporate due diligence and brand protection based on public records.
- Cybersecurity research on public infrastructure (DNS, certificates, archived web pages).
- Academic research and authorized legal or law-enforcement work within the scope of the authorization.

## Prohibited uses

You must not use Angel Engine to:

1. find a private person's home address or other precise private location;
2. track or monitor someone's movements, routine or whereabouts;
3. identify an unknown private individual from their face, or match faces between images;
4. obtain private contact information (personal phone numbers, personal email addresses);
5. stalk, harass, intimidate, threaten, dox or expose anyone;
6. infer sensitive attributes (sexual orientation, religion, ethnicity, health, political views,
   immigration status) about individuals;
7. impersonate anyone or create deceptive accounts or personas;
8. bypass logins, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, privacy settings or other access controls;
9. access leaked, breached, stolen or otherwise restricted data, including credentials;
10. conduct targeted surveillance without legitimate legal authorization;
11. discriminate against people or groups.

## Investigations about individuals

Investigations whose subject is an individual require a stated lawful basis and supervisor approval,
and run in a restricted mode with stricter redaction and narrower sources.

## Enforcement

Requests are screened automatically; refused requests include lawful alternatives. Violations are
logged and reviewed and may lead to suspension of access.

## Reporting misuse

Anyone can report suspected misuse — including people who believe they are being targeted — through the
public **Report abuse** page.
